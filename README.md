# 🧭 Career Compass

> **A Personalized Career Roadmap Generator for Tech Students**

Career Compass is a front-end web application that helps engineering and computer science students discover their ideal tech career path, identify skill gaps, and follow a semester-wise personalized roadmap — all without any backend or login required.

---

## 📸 Overview

Career Compass guides students through a structured self-assessment and instantly generates:

- **Top 3 career matches** based on their interests and current skills
- **Skill gap analysis** highlighting what they already know vs. what they need to learn
- **Career readiness score** displayed as an animated circular progress indicator
- **Semester-wise roadmap** tailored to their current academic year
- **Project suggestions** at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels
- **Progress tracker** to visualize skill acquisition over time

---

| Feature | Description |

## 🗺️ How It Works

1. **Fill the Assessment Form** — Enter your name, degree, branch, year, interests, current skills, and preferred career path.
2. **Get Your Dashboard** — Instantly see your top 3 recommended careers scored by compatibility.
3. **Analyse Skill Gaps** — See exactly which skills you have and which ones are critical to develop.
4. **Check Readiness** — An animated score shows how prepared you are for your target career.
5. **Follow the Roadmap** — A semester-by-semester plan is generated based on your current year.
6. **Pick a Project** — Browse beginner, intermediate, and advanced project ideas to build your portfolio.
7. **Track Progress** — Visualize your skill acquisition with interactive progress bars.
8. **Print or Reset** — Save your roadmap or start a new assessment anytime.

## 📊 Assessment Form Inputs

**Personal Details**
- Full Name, Degree (B.Tech / BCA / MCA / etc.), Branch, Current Year (1–4)

**Interests** *(multi-select)*
- Coding, UI/UX Design, Data Analysis, AI & ML, Web Dev, Mobile Apps, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Research, Problem Solving

**Current Skills** *(multi-select)*
- Python, Java, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, SQL, C/C++, React/Angular, ML Basics, Git & GitHub, Linux, Figma, Statistics & Math

**Career Goal**
- One of the six supported career paths listed above
